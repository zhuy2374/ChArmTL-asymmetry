## Haplotype-Resolved ChArmTL Phasing and Intergenerational Transmission Analysis

This workflow supports the processing and analysis of Chromosome-arm-specific telomere length (ChArmTL) data generated with Telogator2, ranging from batch processing and multi-sample integration to quality control, parental-origin assignment, transmission status determination, and downstream statistical analysis.

A key component of the workflow is inferring the parental origin of offspring ChArmTLs  and consequently the transmission status of parental ChArmTL by comparing telomere variant repeat (TVR) sequence similarity between offspring and parental samples. The inferred assignments undergo manual curation before being integrated into the QC-passed dataset for parent-of-origin and intergenerational transmission analyses.

#### Workflow Overview

The workflow consists of five major stages:

1. **Telogator2 processing and result integration**  
  Batch processing with Telogator2 across all samples and integration of sample-level outputs into a unified dataset.

2. **Parental-origin assignment and transmission status determination**  
  Inferring the parental origin of offspring ChArmTLs and the transmitted/untransmitted status of parental ChArmTLs based on TVR sequence similarity, followed by manual curation.

3. **Quality control and ChArmTL characterization**  
  QC filtering and post-QC characterization of the merged dataset, followed by analyses of ChArmTL rank-order conservation, allelic differences, and sex- and age-related dynamics.

4. **Parent-of-origin asymmetry and independent validation**  
  Comparison of paternally and maternally inherited ChArmTLs within offspring, sibling reproducibility assessment, and external validation in the multi-generational CEPH 1463 pedigree.

5. **Random transmission and parent-offspring correlation**

  Intra-parental comparison of transmitted versus untransmitted ChArmTLs and multi-variable modeling of parent-to-offspring transmission dynamics.

#### Repository Structure

##### run_telogator2

Shell scripts for batch execution of Telogator2 and integration of results generated across multiple samples.

* **`batch_telogator2_jobs.sh`**  
Automates batch processing of a cohort by iterating through a sample list and dynamically generating individual Slurm submission scripts for each sample.

* **`merge_telogator2_results.sh`**  
Aggregates the resulting `tlens_by_allele.tsv` files generated by individual Telogator2 runs into a unified TSV file.

##### tools/similarity_compute

`similarity_compute` is a Python-based tool developed to infer the parental origin of offspring ChArmTLs by quantifying TVR sequence similarity between offspring and parental samples using Telogator2-derived telomere sequence information. 

`similarity_compute` is maintained as an independent Python project, with its own `pyproject.toml` and `uv.lock`.

For installation, usage, input/output requirements, and command-line options, please refer to the dedicated documentation:  
[`tools/similarity_compute/README.md`](https://github.com/zhuy2374/ChArmTL-asymmetry/blob/main/tools/similarity_compute)

##### downstream_analysis

R scripts for data QC, descriptive characterization, statistical modeling, and visualization of ChArmTLs.

* **`01_qc_and_filtering.R`**  
Performs essential quality control and filtering on the raw merged data.

    >**Note:** Parental-origin and transmission status assignments are generated separately using [`tools/similarity_compute`](https://github.com/zhuy2374/ChArmTL-asymmetry/blob/main/tools/similarity_compute). The resulting assignments are manually reviewed and then merged with the QC-passed ChArmTL dataset generated by this script.

* **`02_post_qc_summary.R`**  
Generates summary statistics and visualizations to characterize the ChArmTL dataset after quality control and filtering.

* **`03_rank_order_conservation.R`**  
Evaluates inter-arm heterogeneity, ChArmTL rank-order conservation across family roles, haplotypes, phenotypes, and populations, as well as the rank-order conservation of intra-individual allelic differences (`|Allele1 – Allele2|`).

* **`04_sex_and_age.R`**

  Evaluates sex- and age-related variation in ChArmTLs across chromosome arms, and compares arm-specific effect sizes between paternally and maternally inherited haplotypes.

* **`05_parental_origin_comparison.R`**  
  Compares paternally and maternally inherited ChArmTLs within offspring to assess parent-of-origin asymmetry across chromosome arms and evaluates the reproducibility of arm-specific differences between siblings.

* **`06_validation_in_CEPH-1463.R`**  
  Performs independent external validation using the multi-generational CEPH 1463 pedigree dataset to assess the reproducibility and generalizability of parent-of-origin ChArmTL asymmetry.

* **`07_T_U_comparison_and_intergenerational_correlation.R`**  

  Compares transmitted (`T`) versus untransmitted (`U`) ChArmTLs within fathers and mothers to test for Mendelian segregation (excluding preferential gametogenic transmission), and models parent-to-offspring ChArmTL correlations to characterize dual transmission patterns.
